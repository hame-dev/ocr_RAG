# Brand Image Prompts

Ready-to-copy image generation prompts for the system's two proposed names: **Bayan AI | بيان** and **Daleel AI | دليل**.

Use each prompt separately to generate a distinct brand concept. Keep the NCST company logo out of the product mark and use **© 2026 NCST** as a small presentation footer, separate from the logo.

## 1. Bayan AI | بيان

```text
Create a premium, minimalist logo and brand presentation for “Bayan AI”, with the Arabic name “بيان”.

PRODUCT
An Arabic and English AI platform that reads scanned documents, extracts and organizes information, and answers questions with citations. The brand represents clarity, understanding, and making complex information accessible.

SYMBOL
Design a distinctive geometric mark inspired by lines of text becoming a clear, open page. Use two or three carefully balanced shapes and clever negative space. The result should feel simple, confident, and recognizable at small app-icon sizes. Convey clarity through the geometry itself.

TYPOGRAPHY
Set “Bayan AI” in refined contemporary sans-serif lettering, paired with “بيان” in complementary modern Arabic typography. Preserve correct Arabic spelling, connected letterforms, and natural reading direction. Give both languages equal visual care.

COLOR
Deep petroleum blue #014F7B on a warm white #FAF9F6 background, with a restrained teal accent if needed. Prioritize a design that also works in one color.

COMPOSITION
One finished logo, with the symbol beside the bilingual wordmark, centered on a spacious landscape canvas. Flat, crisp, vector-style artwork with precise edges and generous empty space. Professional, calm, and distinctive.

Place “© 2026 NCST” in very small muted-gray type at the bottom center of the canvas, clearly separate from the logo. Do not include a company logo.

AVOID
Robots, brains, circuits, AI sparkles, decorative calligraphy, tiny details, gradients, shadows, 3D effects, stationery mockups, extra slogans, and multiple competing concepts.
```

## 2. Daleel AI | دليل

```text
Create a premium, minimalist logo and brand presentation for “Daleel AI”, with the Arabic name “دليل”.

PRODUCT
An Arabic and English document intelligence assistant that helps users discover information, ask questions across their documents, and trace answers to the original source. The brand represents guidance, evidence, discovery, and trust.

SYMBOL
Design a distinctive abstract mark combining a document bookmark with a subtle directional pointer. Form it from one strong folded geometric shape, using negative space to suggest a path toward an answer. It should feel purposeful and balanced, with a memorable silhouette that works at small app-icon sizes. Keep the directional reference subtle and sophisticated.

TYPOGRAPHY
Set “Daleel AI” in a contemporary sans-serif wordmark with confident proportions, paired with “دليل” in harmonious modern Arabic typography. Preserve correct Arabic spelling, connected letterforms, and natural reading direction. Align both scripts carefully.

COLOR
Deep petroleum blue #014F7B with a restrained muted-gold #B99A59 accent on a warm white #FAF9F6 background. The core symbol must also work entirely in one color.

COMPOSITION
One finished logo, with the symbol beside the bilingual wordmark, centered on a spacious landscape canvas. Flat, crisp, vector-style artwork. Use generous empty space and precise proportions for an established, professional identity.

Place “© 2026 NCST” in very small muted-gray type at the bottom center of the canvas, clearly separate from the logo. Do not include a company logo.

AVOID
Literal compasses, magnifying glasses, robots, brains, circuit patterns, AI sparkles, elaborate arrows, gradients, shadows, 3D effects, stationery mockups, extra slogans, and multiple competing concepts.
```
